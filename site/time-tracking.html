Time tracking

Your timesheet,
drafted from what you did.

At the end of the day Copit proposes worklogs based on everything that actually happened. You review, adjust and book — to Jira and Tempo, or internally if you don't use Jira at all.

Suggestions

Built from real signals.

The AI turns your day's activity into booking suggestions. It books the estimated effort of a person — not the raw AI time.

  • Agent runs with their tickets and durations
  • Commits mapped to tickets
  • GitLab activity: pushes, merge requests, comments
  • Notes you jotted down during the day

Memory

Asks once, remembers

When something is unclear, Copit asks. Answers like “topic X belongs to ticket Y” are remembered for the following days.

Tempo

Always the right account

Copit only books with a valid Tempo account — taken from the ticket, from memory or from the project mapping, and resolved to the account key Tempo expects.

Epics

Missing ticket? Created.

No fitting ticket for the work? Copit creates a task in the epic, with the account already set.

Calendar

Account status at a glance.

Each day's bar is split by account status — green with an account, orange without, grey unknown — with the hours on top. This includes bookings made outside Copit; accounts are read from Tempo.

In the day view you can add a missing account to an existing booking.
